## 2. What landed today and where it lives

- **Floor tone and light**: `Scripts/UI/Arena/FoundryRegionFinish.cs`. It holds per-flag tone and tint, wider joints and
  stronger bevels (`Paving`), and `FloorLight` built from the real pit, boilers, lanterns and beacons. The soft ceiling
  scales all channels by the brightest one to keep the hue. Its strict bound is 1.74, under the shader's ±2 range.
- **Depth ordering**: [FoundryRegionDepth.cs](../Assets/_Project/Scripts/UI/Arena/FoundryRegionDepth.cs) gives
  structure vertices z = 50 + their ground-diagonal position, and a depth-only pass in `Resources/FoundryCutaway.shader`
  writes it. [FoundryVisibilityView.cs](../Assets/_Project/Scripts/UI/Arena/FoundryVisibilityView.cs) moves actor
  sprites to their feet depth on `Camera.onPreCull` and restores them on `onPostRender`, so gameplay transforms never
  change. Childless actor roots (the chest) get the same treatment. `Scripts/UI/GroundedSorting.cs` keeps the actor
  registry (`Actors`, `ActorOrder` 1).
- **Cutaway**: same shader and view. The colour pass uses ZTest Always, so painter order still decides what is drawn on
  top. The fade applies only to structures in front of the hero's depth and over his body. Flush floor decals are one
  `Foundry floor decals` batch on the plain material at rank −14 (`FoundryRegionView.cs`), so brass lines never fade
  under the hero.
- **X-ray silhouettes**: `Resources/FoundryActorXray.shader`. Pass 1 is Sprites/Default; pass 2 uses ZTest Greater and
  draws an outline and faint fill only where a structure's depth covers the sprite. It uses two shared, scene-owned
  materials: ember for enemies (`EnemyLookView.Body`) and gold for drops (`GroundDropView.cs`) and chests
  (`Scripts/Combat/ChestSpawner.cs` `Body`). The view restores the original materials when disabled and destroys its
  own on reload. The hero gets none.
- **Massing**: `FoundryRegionMassing.cs` holds the boilers, balustrade, pit, foundations, the three pier forms and the
  boiler plate UVs. `FoundryRegionDressing.cs` holds the chains (alternating rings and bars) and the riveted reservoir
  plates. Keep south structures squat: tall ones there made the hero read as standing on a roof.
- **Seal and compass**: `FoundryRegionHeraldry.cs`. Eight-point brass star, a ring pair at r 3.44/3.82, compass rays to
  r 8.6 clipped to the walking union.
- **Bridge**: `FoundryRegionView.cs` `BuildBridgeDeck` plus joints and kerbs in `FoundryRegionSite.cs`. It had three
  passes today: coursed setts, then the court's diagonal grid with rail kerbs, then calmer margins (deck 3,764 → 1,556
  vertices).
- **Rim**: `CopingTrim` and `Builder.MapLast` in `FoundryRegionView.cs` map decal strips onto clean regions of
  `ENV_Platform_Coping.png`, and `BuildCourtBorders` in `FoundryRegionSite.cs` lays a continuous kerb. This removed the
  "vent slot" row.
- **Effect depth**: `Scripts/UI/CombatEffectsView.cs` plus `FoundryVisibilityView.cs`. Above-floor effects store their
  owner's ground y and render at that depth. Floor effects (blast ring, ability pulse) are untouched. Damage, heal and
  gold numbers deliberately stay on top.
- **Texture imports** (meta changes only, GUIDs unchanged):
  - `Art/Props/Foundry/GateFacade.png.meta` and `FurnaceFacade.png.meta`: max size 2048 and `nPOTScale` ToLarger. They
    now ship as 2048×1024 / 1024×2048 ASTC 6×6 at 1.2 MB each; before, each was 3.6 MB of RGBA32.
  - `Art/UI/UI_Panel_ForgedBlank_v2.png.meta`: Android 1024 ASTC 4×4, Default max 1024 (uncompressed). It is now 344.6 KB
    instead of 4.5 MB of RGB24, and the sprite borders are unchanged.
  - Build-report textures: 20.5 MB after the gate fix, 18.1 MB after the furnace fix, 13.9 MB after the panel.
- **The non-power-of-two rule, as observed.** In this Android build, mip-mapped Default-type textures imported with
  `nPOTScale` None shipped as uncompressed RGBA32 despite an ASTC override (the two facades). The six 1254² sources are
  power-of-two only because max size 1024 clamps them to 1024²: never raise their max size without setting `nPOTScale`.
  Sprites ignore `nPOTScale`, and the NPOT UI panel sprite (1024×341, no mips) does ship as ASTC 4×4. Read the build
  report after every import.
- **Capture and tests**: `Tools/ArtReview/FoundryRuntimeCapture.cs` gained a `hud-choice` capture.
  `Tests/PlayMode/FoundryRegionTests.cs` asserts at most 17 static renderers (line 51), exactly one floor-decal batch,
  the x-ray and loot materials, and chest and effect depth apply/restore; `ChestPresentationTests.cs` the ordinary hall.

## 4. Tools and commands

- **Capture:** `pwsh -File Tools/ArtReview/Run-FoundryRuntimeProof.ps1` (about 1 minute).
  - It refuses to start while Unity runs or `Temp/UnityLockfile` exists.
  - It temporarily copies `FoundryRuntimeCapture.cs` into `Tests/PlayMode/` (so nothing else may touch `Assets/` while
    it runs) and runs it as a PlayMode test: seed 42, `floor_density_proof`, foundry preview. It converts BMP to PNG and
    deletes the temporary file.
  - Output always overwrites `ArtDirection/2026-09-23/foundry-playable-01/`. Copy a baseline out first, then copy the
    result into the package's `after/`. That is why each `after/` also carries that folder's 2026-09-23 README and
    device subfolders.
  - `static-budget.txt` holds renderers and vertices, `walk.csv` the traversal. Review points: `start`, `point-0..4`,
    `ground-readability-on/off`, the depth closeups, `overview`, the detail views, `atmosphere-*`, `hud-choice`.
- **Gate, in order, never on a failing step:**
  1. `dotnet test Tools/CombatChecks/CombatChecks.csproj --configuration Release -p:NuGetAudit=false`
  2. `dotnet build Tools/UnityCompileCheck/UnityCompileCheck.csproj`
  3. `pwsh -File Tools/Run-UnityTests.ps1`: EditMode, PlayMode, then the development APK only if both pass.
     `-Platform PlayMode -Filter <full class name> -SkipApk` runs one class.
  4. `pwsh -File Tools/Verify-Project.ps1`

  Run one Unity process at a time (the capture counts as one), and never edit `Assets/` while it is in flight.
- **Parallel agents:** each agent owns whole files. Only the main session runs Unity and adb. Each agent compiles into
  its own output folders so builds do not collide:
  `dotnet build Tools/UnityCompileCheck/UnityCompileCheck.csproj -p:IntermediateOutputPath=<own dir>/obj/ -p:OutDir=<own dir>/bin/`.
- **Phone** (Samsung S23, serial `RFCW20W2WFX`):
  - Before every tap, swipe or `screencap`, and again after a capture, check three things: `com.cryptforge.prototype`
    has focus (`adb shell dumpsys window | findstr mCurrentFocus`), the display is awake (`dumpsys power | findstr
    mWakefulness=`) and no keyguard shows (`dumpsys window | findstr isKeyguardShowing`). Stop the moment focus changes.
    Announce unattended runs; the owner plays on this phone by hand.
