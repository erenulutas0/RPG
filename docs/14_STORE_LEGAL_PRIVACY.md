# Store, Legal & Privacy Checklist

> Not legal advice. Verify requirements at release because store policies change.

## Originality / IP

Do not:
- reuse Bones and Coins assets,
- imitate unique character designs,
- copy UI composition too closely,
- copy names/text,
- market as an unofficial mobile version.

Inspiration should be at the **genre/mechanics level**, with original expression.

## Apple

Relevant current rule:
- randomized virtual items sold through mechanisms such as loot boxes require odds disclosure before purchase.

Apple also requires appropriate in-app purchase handling for digital goods.

## Google Play

Relevant current rule:
- purchased randomized virtual-item mechanisms require clear odds disclosure in close proximity to purchase.

Google Play billing rules apply to digital goods, subject to regional program exceptions.

## Simplest policy-safe MVP

- free download,
- no purchase,
- no paid random loot,
- no account requirement,
- no user-generated content.

This reduces compliance complexity dramatically.

## Privacy

Every SDK may collect data.

Before release:
- inventory SDKs,
- document collected identifiers/events,
- complete Play Data Safety accurately,
- complete Apple privacy disclosures accurately,
- publish privacy policy.

Do not copy another app's privacy answers.

## Analytics

Use only data necessary for product improvement.
Avoid collecting:
- precise location,
- contacts,
- sensitive personal data,
unless genuinely required (it is not required for this game).

## Ads

If ads are enabled:
- verify age rating/audience setup,
- consent requirements by region,
- SDK privacy declarations,
- ad network configuration.

## AI-generated assets

Maintain a provenance sheet:
- tool,
- date,
- prompt/reference,
- human edits,
- source file,
- license/terms version if relevant.

Avoid prompting for copyrighted character imitation.

## Ratings

Expect fantasy/cartoon violence.
Keep blood/gore scope aligned with desired age rating.

## Support

Prepare:
- support email/site,
- privacy policy URL,
- terms if needed,
- deletion flow if accounts are later introduced.
