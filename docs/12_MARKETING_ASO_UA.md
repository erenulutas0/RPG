# Marketing, ASO & User Acquisition

## Marketing starts before release

The project should generate marketability evidence during prototype.

Do not wait for “finished game” to discover that no ad concept communicates the appeal.

## Positioning statement

A compact portrait RPG where players discover absurd hero/weapon synergies during fast dungeon runs and carry meaningful progression between attempts.

## Audience hypotheses

- idle/incremental players,
- survivor/buildcraft players,
- light RPG players,
- pixel-art indie fans,
- players who enjoy “numbers go up” but want real decisions.

## Creative pillars

### 1. Transformation
Weak → broken build.

### 2. Choice
“Which upgrade would you pick?”

### 3. Synergy reveal
An unexpected combo suddenly multiplies power.

### 4. Boss payoff
Boss HP melts after build setup.

### 5. Collection
New hero/weapon unlock.

## Short-form creative templates

### 5-second hook
- show impossible enemy wave,
- show weak hero,
- text: “This build should not work…”

### 15-second
1. problem
2. upgrade choices
3. synergy
4. boss deletion
5. gameplay CTA

### Social devlog
- “Today I added poison that can crit.”
- before/after clips,
- ask viewers to vote between two upgrades.

## Organic channels

Priority:
- TikTok,
- YouTube Shorts,
- X/Twitter gamedev clips,
- Reddit selectively and authentically,
- Discord only after there is an audience to serve.

Do not build a dead Discord first.

## Store page strategy

### Google Play
Google Play supports store listing experiments for text/graphics and custom listings.

Test:
- icon,
- feature graphic,
- first screenshots,
- short description.

### Apple
Apple Product Page Optimization can test up to three alternative product-page versions, including icons/screenshots/previews.
Custom Product Pages can target different audiences/creative themes.

## Screenshot order

1. core combat fantasy
2. build choice
3. hero/weapon variety
4. boss
5. meta progression

Never lead with settings/menu.

## ASO keyword themes

Research at launch using actual store tools:
- idle RPG
- auto battler
- dungeon RPG
- incremental RPG
- pixel RPG
- loot RPG

Do not stuff keywords unnaturally.

## Soft launch country strategy

Choose based on:
- language support,
- CPI,
- device mix,
- target market similarity.

Do not assume a country is “cheap” without current campaign data.

For first organic/closed test, geography matters less than getting relevant players.

## Paid UA discipline

Do not spend meaningfully until:
- onboarding is instrumented,
- retention baseline exists,
- at least 3–5 creative concepts exist.

Small test loop:
1. create 5–10 variations,
2. spend small,
3. measure CTR/CVR/CPI,
4. inspect retention by creative/source,
5. iterate.

Cheap installs with terrible retention are not success.

## Creative production with AI

AI video/image tools can accelerate:
- storyboards,
- concept ads,
- key art,
- variations.

But:
- show real gameplay quickly,
- do not fake mechanics that do not exist,
- keep visual identity consistent.

## Community strategy

During development:
- show mechanics, not only screenshots,
- invite build ideas,
- name upgrades with community later,
- collect testers from people who actually like the genre.

## Launch checklist

- icon A/B candidates,
- 5–8 screenshots,
- 15–30 sec gameplay video,
- privacy policy,
- support email/page,
- analytics verified,
- crash monitoring,
- localized store text for priority markets,
- press/creator one-pager,
- 20+ short-form clips prepared or reproducible.

## Post-launch

Weekly:
- analyze store conversion,
- analyze retention cohorts,
- analyze best/worst creatives,
- ship small content only if core metrics justify,
- collect reviews/feedback themes.
