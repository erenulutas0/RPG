# V0.1's floor length: levers that move it from the gate's wait into fighting (2026-11-04)

The owner's open decision 2 (docs/41, status 2026-11-03 night and 2026-11-04). The floor holds its Warden gate until the
floor clock reads 3:30, so in a clear about 110 s is the wait and the gate, not the fighting, carries the 4-6 minutes.
This note measures the levers that could carry the length with fighting instead, and puts together three lever sets for
the owner to choose from. **The choice is the owner's.** Nothing shipped changes: every value here sits behind the foundry
simulation's levers (`DescentSimulation.FoundryLevers`), whose defaults are the shipped floor (Floor_EmberHalls: surges 2,
one extra elite, the gate at 210 s).

Branch `claude/research-v01-levers-1104`, from `origin/research/pilot-cohorts` (the careful pilot and its cohorts).
Simulation only, with no Editor or device run. Seeds 1-200 for each hero. Every lever is run by all five cohorts of the
autopilot.

## How it was measured

- `FOUNDRY_LEVERS=4` runs the single levers (`FoundryBatch.GateLevers`) and `FOUNDRY_LEVERS=5` runs the lever sets
  (`FoundryBatch.GateLeverSets`). Each lever is played by every cohort on the same seeds and written to
  `foundry-gate-levers.md` and `.csv` in `FOUNDRY_OUT`. The single levers cover floor 1 only. The sets go on to floor 2
  (`FOUNDRY_FLOORS=2`), so the last cohort column of the sets table reads both floors.
- New levers, all off on the shipped floor (`DescentSimulation.Foundry.cs`):
  - `GateKillQuota` and `GateOpenLevel`: the gate also opens once the floor's kills, or the hero's level, reach a value,
    whichever comes first beside the clock. With the clock at 0 the gate waits for the quota or the level alone, and opens
    at the 300 s overrun at the latest.
  - `GateSiegeHall`: while the gate holds, the floor's n-th combat hall sends its waves again in turn (a "hold the gate"
    mini-event).
  - `HordeTargetGrowth`: how much the horde's standing crowd grows by 300 s (`HordeClock.TargetGrowth`, 0.5). It runs
    through `TunedHordeDirector`, which calls the game's own body at the shipped growth.
- New accounting, the same on every lever:
  - What fills the wait: the share of it the hero is engaged (the gate's engaged rule), and whether the steer kites, walks
    to a drop, fights a node wave, walks elsewhere or stands still.
  - Over the wait: kills, levels gained and health lost.
  - "Ready": the floor clock, kills and level at the moment the room before the gate clears.
- Existing levers used: `HallSurges`, `ExtraHalls`, `ExtraElites`, `HallHoldSeconds` (a hall's waves repeat until it has
  run that long), `HordeTargets`, `HordeExperienceShare` and `GateNotBeforeSeconds`.
- Targets (docs/41 step 2 and the V0.1 gate table, read for the Vanguard):
  - floor P50 240-360 s over every run;
  - combat uptime >= 0.75;
  - engaged uptime >= 0.75 (the lead's stricter, proposed reading);
  - first combat P90 <= 10 s;
  - Warden fight P50 45-65 s.
- Further limits from the brief: the gate's wait under about 20 s, the careful cohort's clears no higher, and the clumsy
  cohort's not below about 20 %.

### What fills the wait today: a safe farm

The cohort clears the room before the gate at a P50 floor clock of 1:44 (Vanguard) and 1:34 (Ilya), then waits
106-116 s. Over the wait, in the careful clears:

- **92 % of the wait the autopilot walks from drop to drop.** It kites 0 %, never fights a node wave, and stands with
  nothing to do 7 %.
- It is engaged 68 % (Vanguard) and 47 % (Ilya) of the wait. The horde keeps coming and dies at the hero's feet.
- It kills about 310-345 enemies and **gains 11-12 levels** (14 -> about 25), for **about 1 health lost**.

So the wait is neither idle nor a fight: it is a risk-free XP farm, and the Warden is fought on its levels. The clumsy
cohort shows this most. With the gate's time removed, the clumsy Vanguard's clears fall from 37 % to 27 %, and its deaths
at the Warden double (20 -> 42). The careful cohort barely notices (99-100 %).

The gate table's engaged-uptime row was "not met" before any lever, and the wait drags it down.

### Reading the single levers

- **The gate's time is the only lever that moves the floor without fighting.** Each second off the gate takes about a second
  off the floor (3:30 -> 3:00: 299 -> 270 s). At no gate time a clear is 196 / 175 s, under the band for both heroes.
- **Behind the 3:30 gate, content mostly turns into a shorter wait, not a longer floor.**
  - +2 halls: the wait drops 106 -> 67 s, the floor moves +3 s.
  - Surges 4: the wait drops to 88 s, the floor moves 0 s.
  - Halls held 45 s: the wait drops to 30 s, the floor moves -2 s.
  - So any fighting lever needs the gate brought forward with it.
- **Fight content that lengthens the floor, with no gate time:**

  | Lever | Floor (V / I) | Clumsy clears (V / I) |
  |---|---|---|
  | +1 hall | +24 / +29 s | 6 % / 16 % (the clumsy cohort's Warden deaths rise 20 -> 72) |
  | +2 halls | +48 / +51 s | 20 % / 54 % |
  | +3 halls | +74 / +73 s | 12 % / 42 % |
  | Halls held 30 s | +30 / +36 s | 14 % / 42 % |
  | Halls held 45 s | +66 / +76 s | 10 % / 22 % |
  | Surges 4 | +13 / +14 s | - |
  | Surges 6 | +31 / +33 s | - |
  | A third elite | +14 / +20 s | 4 % / 6 % (the Warden is reached on fewer levels) |

  Halls and holds lengthen the floor most per second of fighting. The holds and surges also raise engaged uptime
  (0.75-0.78).
- **A denser or faster-growing horde does not lengthen the floor.** Horde growth 1.5 or 3, or targets x2, leave the floor
  and the time to the gate within 3 s. The horde does not slow the hero between nodes, and kills what it brings. It is a
  feel lever, not a length lever.
- **A kill-quota gate is a time gate in disguise.** Over the wait the kills come at the farm's rate, about 3 a second:
  - 400 kills is about gate 2:30;
  - 600 kills is about gate 3:30;
  - 800 kills is about gate 4:30.

  The wait is still 92 % drops. It changes what the screen says, not what the hero does.
- **A level gate (3:30 or level 22/24, or level 20 alone)** shortens the wait for runs that level faster, but the wait is
  still the farm. "All halls cleared" is the same as no gate time, because this floor's halls come in order before the gate.
- **A siege fills the wait with fighting.**
  - Hall 1's waves at the 3:30 gate: the wait is 54 % node waves and 39 % drops.
  - Engaged uptime reaches 0.75 / 0.64; with hall 3's waves, 0.78 / 0.69.
  - It costs the clumsy cohort heavily over a 105 s wait: clumsy clears 15 % / 43 % with hall 1, 6 % / 12 % with hall 3.
  - It is a good filler for a short leftover wait, not a substitute for the floor's content.

## Three lever sets for the owner

Every set is only a set of values behind the simulation's levers, and each one needs a scene implementation and an Editor
run (HordePacingMeasurement, seeds 11-66) before a decision. "Met" means the V0.1 gate table's status for the Vanguard.

### Set A: two more halls, three surges, the gate at 2:45, horde XP 0.25

`ExtraHalls 2, HallSurges 3, GateNotBeforeSeconds 165, HordeExperienceShare 0.25`

- **Length from fighting.**
  - A clear is 264 / 250 s, and 260 / 239 s less its wait.
  - The wait is 1 / 11 s P50; only 111 / 194 of the clears wait at all.
  - The room before the gate clears at about 2:44 / 2:34 at level 25 / 24, the level the farm used to give.
- **Gate statuses.**
  - Floor time: met.
  - Engaged uptime: not met, 0.72 / 0.62 against shipped 0.71 / 0.56.
  - Warden: 65 / 58 s, at the top of the 45-65 window; the status reads not met at the Vanguard's 65.x.
  - Clock at clear P90: 247 / 227 s.
- **Clears.** Careful 99 / 100 %. Clumsy 23 % / 49 % (shipped 37 / 80). Both floors, clumsy 15 % / 32 % (shipped 21 / 54).
- **Content cost.** Two more halls. On the map these reuse the four districts, so halls 5 and 6 revisit districts 4 and 1
  (`FoundryMap.RoomSite` cycles), with copies of halls 2 and 3's waves. Hall 3 also gets one more surge (the
  `FloorDefinition` field). The XP share is a one-constant change in `HordeRule`, which reaches every floor.
- **Feel and risk.**
  - The floor is node fights rather than a long horde stretch, as docs/40's node-fight shape asks.
  - The hero revisits districts, which may read as filler without new hall layouts.
  - The clumsy Ilya drops the most (80 -> 49 %): more hall exposure at lower levels, and the farm is gone.
  - Variant R2 without the extra surge (surges 2): clumsy 23 % / 61 %, but a 22 / 32 s wait.

### Set B: halls held 30 s, three surges, the gate at 2:45, horde XP 0.25

`HallHoldSeconds 30, HallSurges 3, GateNotBeforeSeconds 165, HordeExperienceShare 0.25`

- **Length from fighting.**

  - Floor time: met.
  - Engaged uptime: 0.74 / 0.63, one hundredth short.
  - Warden: 62 / 53 s, met.
  - Clock at clear P90: 234 / 224 s.
- **Clears.** Careful 100 / 100 %. Clumsy 23 % / 54 %. Both floors, clumsy 20 % / 41 %, the closest of the three to
  shipped.
- **Content cost.** No new rooms. It needs a new rule: a hall objective timer, under which a hall's waves repeat until the
  hall has run 30 s (the simulation's `HallHoldSeconds`; `EncounterController` would need it), plus a hall timer on the HUD.
- **Feel and risk.**
  - A hall that keeps sending waves until its timer runs out is close to Megabonk's timed objectives, but it can read as
    stalling on a fast build.
  - Gate 2:30 brings the wait to 10-16 s but the floor P50 to 234-236 s, just under the band (rows R2). The owner can

  - Whatever wait is left becomes a fight, so no run stands at a closed door.

### What none of them meets

- **Engaged uptime >= 0.75 together with the 240 s floor.** The best is 0.74. The engaged rule counts an enemy within the
  first weapon's reach, and walks between halls lower it whatever fills the floor. Reaching it probably needs the rule's
  owner to re-read it (for example, the walks apart) or shorter walks between districts.
- **The clumsy cohort's shipped clears.** Every set costs the clumsy Vanguard 14-17 points and the clumsy Ilya 26-31,
  because the farm was its safety margin. Holding it at the shipped 37 / 80 would need more XP pay-back. XP 0.3 did not
  help Set A's clumsy cohort over 0.25 (23 / 49), so its deaths are in the halls, not the level. Or it would need easier
  hall waves for the extra halls.
