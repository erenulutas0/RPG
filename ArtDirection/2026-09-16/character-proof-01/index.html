What this proof decides

  1. Readability: recognize the blue hero quickly at source scale, and distinguish a Mite's body from its flame.
  2. Occlusion: watch the two Mites at the upper-right. Their foot points are 54 source pixels apart vertically: the projection of 0.9 floor units at the current camera width.
  3. Production: test a reduced sprite sample before choosing a denser pixel grid. The generated masters are painterly and antialiased.
Next: fixed foot pivots, separate equipment layers, opposing facings and a small idle / movement / strike sequence. These still images do not prove frame consistency or weapon alignment.
