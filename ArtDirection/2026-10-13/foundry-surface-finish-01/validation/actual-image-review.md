# Actual Unity image review

Root independently inspected the fresh After full map, court, gate, central portrait, shaft and bridge captures, comparing the court and gate directly with their matched Before views. The independent reviewer inspected six full Before/After pairs, including the forecourt portrait; see independent-visual-review.md. These are paused production-scene Editor captures with HUD hidden, not physical-device screenshots.

## Accepted changes

- The floor no longer has dense pale concrete-like aggregate. Slate strata and quiet mineral masses are more coherent with large mesh-authored flags. Existing joints, contour wear, brass inlay and warm local light remain legible. The slight increase in average floor value is accepted at unchanged .8 material tint; it does not compete with the hero, enemy glow or chest in the reviewed portrait.
- No conspicuous extra straight repeat boundary is visible in the inspected views. The source is not mathematically edge-matched, so this is bounded visual acceptance of these actual views, not a claim of perfect seamlessness or motion acceptance.
- Gate coping and broad courses read as connected stone above the painted door. The darker vertical band remains readable as a recess/contact beneath lighter coping. The underlying forge-light relationships remain intact. Existing geometry rather than additional ornaments carries the change.
- The active texture report confirms FoundryPavingV6 at 1024x1024, ASTC 6x6, 11 mip levels, non-readable. Region cost is 32 renderers and 159454 vertices versus 158653 (+801); distant geometry remains 3572. These counts are not FPS measurements.

## Remaining reference gap

This is an incremental material improvement, not reference parity. The broad floor remains relatively regular. Landmark stone and boiler surfaces still have more obvious low-poly facets and simpler weathering than the painted assets and approved concept. Next art work should target authored material variation and worn construction at a selected landmark rather than adding uniform noise or more small props. Preserve the quiet combat floor.

Bounded masonry UV groups still reduce sampling density when a face exceeds the known clean atlas patch; global uniform texel density is not claimed. Real phone motion, HUD contrast, shimmer and midrange performance remain pending. No further source adjustment is required for this package's Editor acceptance.
