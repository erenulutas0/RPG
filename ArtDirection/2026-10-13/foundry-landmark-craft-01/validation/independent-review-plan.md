# Independent landmark-craft acceptance plan - 2026-10-13

Baseline: f0ce770 and the accepted entrance-craft package. The reviewer read the recent authoritative art handoff and compared its actual full-map, gate-detail, yard-detail and forecourt-detail images with the approved foundry-v1 reference.

## Direction

The largest remaining local mismatch at the gate is the plain mass and shallow visual framing of the stone piers beside the already detailed painted door. A useful change must make the niche frame read as carved stone with an actual reveal, while retaining the existing gate massing, door and broad quiet surfaces. Adding more trim around the door or brightening every stone edge would increase the mismatch.

The working apron can gain credible use through muted settled scale at actual receiving and stock contacts, reduced along a plausible passing corridor. The central court and medallion should retain their calm material. This should remain a local surface treatment, not a new route marking or general darkening of the map.

## Ownership and source checks

- GateRecesses and its existing tests: replace the original flat inner-frame geometry with shallow inward splay, connect shortened returns exactly to the same backing, and preserve outer frame, shaft silhouette, caps and bearings. Confirm backing remains visible and no hidden full wall, duplicated face or gap is introduced. Grain remains bounded away from atlas joints and noncollapsed on rendered triangles.
- ServiceAprons and its existing tests: alter only the colours of the apron vertices emitted by the active production branch. Preserve vertex positions, triangle ownership, world UVs, allocation counts and existing selected-parent coverage. Multiply the current colour by a scalar so forge-light RGB relationships remain intact. Require actual referenced samples in the intended operation contacts and neutral behaviour outside the authored footprint.

## Actual-image acceptance

Gate-detail and approach-20-1 must show a visibly more carved frame with connected reveals and an open dark backing. Passing geometry tests alone is insufficient if the splay remains invisible. Reject filled-looking niches, projected slivers, dark cracks, uniform bright outlines or added decorative clutter.

Yard, stock, casting and full-map views must show broad use anchored to the machinery and a readable but subdued passing corridor. Reject repeating stained tiles, circular dirt stamps, constant-width dark stripes, conspicuous patch borders or tonal changes to the central fighting field. Full-map and portrait actor contrast must remain stable.

The final review will use the fresh matched twenty-view capture. Editor captures establish composition and material continuity; they do not establish device performance, shimmer or interactive acceptance.
