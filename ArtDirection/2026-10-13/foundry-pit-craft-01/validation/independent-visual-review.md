## Stone guard

The coping now reads as a broad fitted stone boundary. The old repeating streak-like patches on its narrow band are reduced, leaving quieter mineral variation and intentional joints. The whole pit outline stays restrained; it has not become a bright dashed rim. Posts remain seated, real upper openings remain visible, and the shaft's near/far depth relationship is preserved in the reviewed views.

## Hood surfaces and repaired defect

The first candidate had a conspicuous flat navy annulus against the warmer shell and neck, especially on the larger left hood. The reviewer rejected that material discontinuity and traced it to the exterior cap retaining its old unrelated color literal. The final capture removes the annulus: head and shoulder now read as one formed shell, with deliberate dark hold-down straps and a separate dark flue interior.

The broad body no longer alternates between conspicuous blue-green facet stripes. Its material has a warmer brown/olive cast than the earlier version. A cooler iron response remains an optional direction for later refinement, but the current surface is internally coherent with the forge-lit vessel below and is not a clear regression requiring another edit. It should not be described as photorealistic metal or a match to the reference's complete industrial finish.

In the portrait approach, the hero and attack guide stay clear and the machinery remains a secondary landmark. Court-detail retains clear combat actors and chest. The floor, gate hierarchy and overall map silhouette show no unintended material change in these reviewed pairs.

## Remaining reference gaps

The reference still has richer edge construction, localized contact shadows, stone wear and integrated material transitions. The native hood straps and vessel surfaces are simpler and flatter than the painted industrial portions. Pit fire remains a stylized bright boundary. The broader architecture and floor arrangement remain cleaner and more regular than the reference. These are subsequent art-direction tasks, not reasons to add clutter or globally increase exposure in this package.

Still images do not establish motion shimmer, phone compression in movement, full-HUD touchscreen readability, frame time, or midrange performance. The reviewed package is an Editor visual acceptance only.
