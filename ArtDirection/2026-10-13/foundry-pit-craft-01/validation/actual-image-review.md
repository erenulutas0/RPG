# Actual image review

Root inspected the corrected shaft-detail, approach--20-6 and full-map captures, comparing the shaft directly with the fresh Before image and the rejected initial image.

- The coping no longer carries a repeated narrow diagonal metal-like stripe. Broad stone sections have quieter joint and edge variation, with real openings still exposing the pit lining and fire below.
- The corrected head caps now connect to their shoulders without the initial flat navy annulus. Dark straps and mouth interiors remain readable. Angular reflection is smoother across the old facets; local warm forge illumination and mottled grain remain visible.
- The resulting hood finish is warm, muted cast metal under the pit light; it is not a shiny neutral-steel finish. At the portrait camera the machinery stays behind the hero and the traversable apron remains clear.
- In the full-map view, the pit retains its existing position, size and light hierarchy. This is a local craft improvement rather than a new composition or an enlargement of the map.

Accepted for this bounded package. The concept reference still has richer material variation, industrial detailing and atmosphere. Actual on-device motion, shimmer, HUD visibility, crowd overlap and midrange performance remain unverified. See independent-visual-review.md for the separate reviewer's assessment.
