# Basalt material source

Built-in image_gen; no CLI/API fallback. Generated source copied unchanged to basalt-v1.png and Assets/_Project/Art/Props/Foundry/FoundryPaving.png. No raster edits.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: opaque square game material albedo texture for the top of large basalt paving slabs in an original ancient arcane foundry. Entire image filled edge to edge with ONE continuous dark cool charcoal/slate-grey stone surface. Orthographic directly overhead, no perspective, neutral even diffuse illumination, no cast shadows. Painterly high-quality game texture, broad softly worn mineral patches and occasional fine shallow fissures, polished footsteps wear, restrained low contrast, no noisy stippling. Medium-dark rather than black, nearly neutral grey with a slight blue undertone. No tile grid, no joints, no grout, no individual slab border, no frame, no bevel drawn at image edge, no objects, no lava, no orange, no metal, no symbols, no lettering. This will be mapped onto modeled individual paving stones and lit in Unity, so do not bake scenery, vignette or directional lighting. Seamlessly tiling appearance, consistent material at all edges.
