# Next reference-layout candidate

Read-only diagnosis, 2026-10-12. Compared the actual `foundry-court-hierarchy-01/after/full-map.png` with approved `2026-09-23/foundry-layout-01/foundry-v1.png`; checked `FoundryMap`, bridge/site support anchors and capture setup. No Assets changes or runtime execution.

The largest remaining structural mismatch is the distribution of the main deck. The reference gives the central ceremonial court the dominant broad foreground, with a subordinate western working ledge. Our deck still reads as a long diagonal yard-to-gate bar: the western working field competes with the court. Better joints or a larger compass cannot change that silhouette.

This is partly presentation, but not only camera framing. The current full-map capture uses a fixed orthographic size of 37 at (0,2,-10), 1600x1200; the concept uses another composition and cannot supply calibrated world dimensions. Source geometry nevertheless establishes the imbalance: the unclipped main deck spans u=-36..14 and v=-14..18 (50 by 32). The strip west of the pit is 20 units; clear depth below the pit is 16, and the eastern court strip is 18. These are bounding-strip measurements, not unobstructed widths everywhere. The pit occupies [-16,-4] by [2,14]. Zooming changes apparent size, not these relationships.

One bounded next candidate: **redistribute four units from the west edge to the forecourt**, testing `West=-32` and `South=-18` together; retain East, North, pit, gate landing and bridge endpoints. The main bounding rectangle becomes 46 by 36; west-of-pit and below-pit strips become 16 and 20 respectively. This preserves the sum of the two plan spans, hence the unclipped projected horizontal span, while allocating more floor to the entrance/court side. These are trial design coordinates, not measured reference dimensions. Keep the same comparison camera first.

Treat this as one shared-layout package, not an art-only stretch. `Rooms`, corner planes, surface/movement/segment tests, pursuit and spawning must agree. Re-seat west assemblies and bridge-head architecture using their derived offsets/seams; audit hard-coded rails, inlays, equipment and exterior supports. Preserve the fixed pit, gate ramp, material batches, world grain and flush floor. Rebuild matching outboard foundations and depth prisms so no scenery occupies newly walkable ground and no removed floor retains floating supports. Do not blanket-scale meshes or camera.

Acceptance requires matched full-map and actor-height captures plus boundary, bridge-join, movement, pursuit and separated-spawn regression checks. Current screenshots support this diagnosis and the modest gain from the latest apron; they do not prove the proposed layout, reference parity, crowd circulation or phone performance. No implementation or acceptance is implied by this note.
