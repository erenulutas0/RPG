# Fortress masonry actual-image review

2026-10-12. The fortress author inspected actual matched Before/After `full-map`, `bridge-detail` and `court-detail` captures and the approved `2026-09-23/foundry-layout-01/foundry-v1.png` reference. This is author review, not independent fortress acceptance or a phone observation.

**Accept the local fortress/bridge material package. No concrete blocking visual artifact was found.**

- In the bridge detail, the lower wall and arch faces read as broad cool stone rather than the former stretched dark streaks. Their grain is visible and restrained. There is no new pale stripe, conspicuously repeating small tile or blank untextured band in the inspected faces.
- The larger fitted courses keep a masonry bond while reducing the thin repetitive horizontal divisions. The narrow arch returns stay darker than the front planes. The original structural contact below the deck and the pier/arch-foot overlaps still appear seated; no bright crack or open seam was visible.
- The bridge's existing straight coping and profile remain connected at the court edge. Statues, consoles, hanging banners, lanterns and chains retain their attachment and layer order in the close capture. The bridge mouth remains open and the lower front arch is still a void.
- At full-map scale the cooler, clearer lower planes modestly strengthen the platform's continuous mass. They remain below the court/fire/boiler visual hierarchy and do not become a luminous blue perimeter. Adjacent painted boiler foundations and drains retain their placement; this is improved value/material cohesion, not a replacement of their source art.
- The court detail keeps the same readable actors, feet, blue guide and chest. The changed architectural faces stay beneath/outside the playing surface. This static view does not prove moving actor/camera or crowd behavior.

The whole-map gain is modest. The reference remains richer in construction variety, wear, integrated shadows and painted material detail; this package does not resolve that full gap. Some lower faces are still fairly uniform compared with the reference, and the broader course treatment intentionally keeps them quiet. Device shimmer, moving-edge occlusion and performance remain unverified here.
