# Service skyline actual-image review

2026-10-12. Skyline author visually inspected matched Before/After `service-shaft-detail`, `skyline-detail`, and `full-map`, plus the approved `2026-09-23/foundry-layout-01/foundry-v1.png`. These are actual root Unity captures. No Assets, Unity, ADB or Git operations were performed for this review.

## Result

Accept the shortened skyline as a visible, bounded hierarchy improvement. No blocking gap, detached head or new island/actor occlusion was found in these views.

- **Height change is visible:** both crowns are clearly lower in the matched close and whole-map views. The larger shaft exposes more of the atmospheric background above it; the smaller shaft is better scaled to the crane/loading assembly. This is a real silhouette change, not a darker replacement hidden in the background.
- **Connected upper profile:** lower shoulders are less pyramidal. Recessed necks continue into the hollow crowns without a visible gap, and the open exhaust rims remain intact. The two shafts retain unequal sizes and recognizable industrial shapes.
- **Arch:** both broken halves descend with their supporting shafts. Their outer ends still enter the corresponding tower masses; no floating gap at the visible contacts was found. The central void remains clearly open. The broken masonry arms still look rigid and simplified in the close view, which remains a reference-quality limitation rather than a new attachment defect.
- **Whole-map hierarchy:** the western service edge draws less attention away from the broad playable surface and rich gate/boiler assembly. The loading equipment and adjacent pit are easier to read as local work areas. The existing court outline and gate location have not changed.
- **Gameplay vicinity:** the visible hero/guide at the service detail stays clear. The shortened background structures do not cover the loading props, rails or playable floor in the inspected captures.

## Limits

This is author review, not independent review of my own implementation. The reference still has richer, less regular industrial masonry, denser construction relationships, and a different court/yard composition. The remaining shafts are still visibly native geometric forms against a richer painted background. Their new scale helps, but full reference parity is not claimed. Static review does not establish motion, shimmer, crowd/HUD, device exposure or performance acceptance.
