# Independent gate material source review

2026-10-12. Reviewed by the pit-material helper, independent of the gate implementation. Read `FoundryRegionGateRecesses.cs`, its original tier/stone-patch helpers, existing gate-recess tests and the gate author's source note. Visually inspected the actual `FoundryMasonryV4.png`; confirmed its GUID is used by `MAT_FoundryMasonry.mat`.

**No blocking source defect found.** Acceptance here is source-only; root still owns compilation, tests and actual captures.

- `GateRecessCoordinates` first solves normalized height using the lower edge and left side, then recovers width on the interpolated lower/upper span. This is valid for the actual tier's trapezoids: their upper and lower edges remain parallel after the local affine floor projection, while the width changes with height. It avoids using a rectangular inverse across the taper. Clamping bounds color lookup for inset-return vertices. This is a local tier helper, not a general inverse for arbitrary bilinear quadrilaterals.
- The backing's own depth gradient removes the `inward` displacement before evaluating its height. The outer light-restoration pass uses the projected vertex on the original face basis, so the recess receives the nearby shell's bounded floor-relative light. The depth offset only shifts that bounded interpolation; it cannot introduce unbounded extrapolation.
- `GateRecessGrain` bounds UVs to approximately x .035-.398 and y .438-.567. Those coordinates lie inside the broad left stone in the atlas's middle row, clear of the actual mortar edges. The square atlas makes its single projected scale isotropic. The backing shares one mapping across all five triangles; the dressed center and bevels share one mapping per stone. This removes stretched/restarted square patches without introducing a painted masonry seam.
- Existing emitted points, face ordering and triangle topology are preserved. The new wrappers rewrite UVs/colors only. Backing, connected depth returns, broad frame, exact corner and cap remain, with no new geometry, material or renderer.
- Color interpolation factors are clamped, the light ratio has a positive denominator floor, and final alpha is explicitly 1 throughout the shaft loop and copied cap/corner path. Positive authored shade/tints keep RGB finite and nonnegative; HDR RGB values are not incorrectly clamped to alpha bounds.
- The backing remains intrinsically darker than its frame, with its upper tint further reduced. Its numerical gain increase does not remove real recess depth or return directionality. Whether that produces enough separation from the formerly black backing is an actual-image question, not guaranteed by the source.

Existing six recess tests continue to guard projected coverage, open throat, original tapered silhouette, copied cap/corner and bounded vertex cost. Their assertions were not weakened. No source edits, Unity, compilation, ADB or Git actions were performed during this independent review.
