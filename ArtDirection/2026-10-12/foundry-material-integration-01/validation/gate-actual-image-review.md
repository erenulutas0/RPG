# Gate actual-image review

2026-10-12. Gate material helper visually inspected all six matched images: Before and After `gate-detail`, `approach-20-1`, and `full-map`. These are root's Unity captures, not phone screenshots. No Assets, Unity, ADB or Git operations were performed for this review.

## Result

Accept as a bounded material improvement. No concrete blocking defect was found in the inspected images.

- **Recess depth:** the formerly near-black backs now show stone grain. Dark heads and side returns still separate the backing from the front surround, while the broad sill remains visible. The near and far visible niches continue to read as blind recesses rather than open background holes or uniformly bright patches. They are less stark than Before; they have not become washed-out panels.
- **Surround grain:** tall jamb and buttress faces now carry finer, more evenly scaled mineral texture. The broad dressed pieces are visibly less plain. No new horizontal mortar stripe, conspicuous UV stretch or bevel grain discontinuity was apparent in the close capture.
- **Hierarchy and attachment:** the door's painted metal, gold lock, blue banners and flames remain stronger accents. Cap, cornice, pipe and sill contacts remain visually seated. The new surface has not brightened the entire gate or changed its silhouette.
- **Portrait:** the hero's feet and blue guide remain clear on the gate approach. This portrait crops most upper niches, so it is evidence of preserved nearby readability, not a complete moving-camera recess test.
- **Whole map:** improvement is local and modest at full-map scale. The gate retains its prior mass and value relationship to the court. This material slice does not fix the broader composition and native-versus-painted construction differences recorded in the separate baseline priorities note.

## Limits

The native stone is still simpler and more regular than the approved reference, especially adjacent cap/chamfer surfaces outside this narrow shaft treatment. Static views cannot establish motion shimmer, HUD/crowd readability, phone exposure or performance. Those checks remain separate. This is author review of the gate treatment, not an independent review of my own source.
