## 1. Court, western yard and gate composition as one assembly

The reference reads as a broad central court with a distinct western loading apron and a raised gate approach. The baseline reads more like one long diagonal slab: the pit sits within that continuous field, the western service silhouette is dominated by two tall plain shafts, and the gate is a narrow terminal across its upper end. The same landmarks exist, but their relative masses and negative spaces still convey a different place.

Candidate package: coordinate the western service massing, pit-to-court visual boundary and gate-approach shoulders at the existing footprints. Reduce the visual dominance of the tall service shafts and make the crane/loading apron, open court and upper approach three legible linked zones. Use substantial connected foundations and architectural transitions, not more isolated posts or trim. Keep the actual combat space clear.

Review criteria: compare with the island normalized to the same on-screen extent, then inspect a grayscale thumbnail. The broad open court should be the main plane; the pit, yard and gate should remain distinct landmarks without the western shafts overpowering them. The reference and current captures have different framing, so no numerical width ratio is inferred here. If the normalized comparison still requires a wider walkable outline, record that as an explicit layout decision; more surface detail cannot supply missing width.

## 2. Cohesion of native fortress mass and painted industrial assets

The reference has a continuous stone fortress: parapets, supports and lower walls share dressed stone scale, shaded contacts and irregular mineral wear. In the baseline, the crisp native outer walls, bridge supports and tall shafts retain large flat or regular geometric planes, while the painted boiler bodies and door contain much richer localized shading. The far environment is also very imposing around the relatively clean playable island. This makes individual assets look assembled rather than quarried and built together.

Candidate package: a coherent outer-wall/bridge/service-body material and contact-light pass, judged together with adjacent painted boiler foundations. Reuse existing surfaces and batches, replacing stretched or oversized texture sampling and abrupt value breaks before adding geometry. Establish broader cool masonry planes, restrained warm light only near actual sources, and convincing shared contact shade at heavy attachments. Preserve a quiet floor for actors.

Review criteria: at both full-map and actor-height scale, the bridge, outer fortress and boilers should share plausible material density and exposure. No repeating mortar bands, uniformly lit faces, floating bright foundation joins or newly crushed black walls. The island silhouette must separate from the distant structures without increasing orange brightness everywhere. This package should improve the whole assembly, not only a close crop; motion/shimmer and phone acceptance remain separate checks.

The present court/pit/gate material slice may improve some of these surfaces. Its actual After images must determine the next scope before more implementation begins.
