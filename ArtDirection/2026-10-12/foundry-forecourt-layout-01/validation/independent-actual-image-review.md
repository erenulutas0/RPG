## Composition

The new near edge gives the entrance a noticeably deeper central forecourt. The arrival bridge's exposed portion is shorter, and its keepers now announce a court rather than a long stem attached to an oversized western extension. The west yard remains recognisable through its crane, two equipment islands and transfer ring, but its mass no longer extends as far left of the pit. The pit and gate remain fixed landmarks, preserving the reference's overall west-working-yard / inset-furnace / central-court / raised-gate relationship.

The result is a useful change of proportions, rather than an overall zoom or an indiscriminately larger island. It is closer to the reference in entrance-to-court hierarchy. It does not finish the reference match: the prototype still has a very regular paving field, a comparatively thin/simple compass, sparse court-edge dressing and restrained/regular architectural massing. Those are subsequent art refinements, not reasons to revert the present layout.

## Contacts and circulation appearance

- In the matched court view, the hero, enemy bodies, chest and ability guide remain legible on the same floor. The moved foreground wall gives the entrance side of this composition more breathing room. No new floor gap or paving discontinuity is visible.
- In the yard view, the transfer ring and approach rails move coherently with the equipment. The bench and trolley remain seated on their raised foundations; none appears to hover or intersect the pit collar. The west rail still reaches its receiving side.
- In the crane view, the jib, sheave, hanging load, masonry shelf and supporting structure translate together. The crop changes because the camera is matched, not because the assembly loses a part. The full-map view confirms the complete relocated assembly and clear space toward the interior islands.
- In the bridge/forecourt views, both court joins stay open. The keeper bases remain seated; the banners and new lantern rows are visually separate. There is no new cross-lane wall, roof over the walking lane, or detached support visible. The closer below-deck bridge bents remain below the deck.
- South bastions meet the shifted rim. The relocated hanging chain has its upper contact at an iron plate on the near masonry; the other chains stay attached to their bastion or bridge-foot masses in these views. No obsolete chain is left hanging from the former south line.
