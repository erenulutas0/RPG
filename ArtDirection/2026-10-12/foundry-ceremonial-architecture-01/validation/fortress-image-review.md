# Fortress actual-image review — ceremonial architecture

Reviewed 2026-10-12 after the root reported the 16-view actual Unity capture passing. This is an image review, not a substitute for the root's mesh tests or device verification. Assets remained frozen.

## Evidence inspected

- Matched `before` / `after` images: `full-map.png`, `bridge-detail.png`, `forecourt-detail.png`.
- Approved composition reference: `ArtDirection/2026-09-23/foundry-layout-01/foundry-v1.png`.

## Findings

**No blocking visual regression found in these views.**

- The lower fortress now has a legible primary/secondary treatment: the broad stone faces retain their weight, while secondary vertical panels and shallow arches sit behind the dressed primary structure. This is visible at the forecourt's right wall and the walls flanking the entrance. The top parapet remains seated on the same body, with no exposed background crack at the join.
- The nearest bridge bearing reads as a single horizontal stone course above its dressed arch. I do not see a doubled strip or a second overprinted stone edge at the partition. The lower arch aperture is still open in the full-map view; neither the bearing course nor the recessed farther bent introduces a new closure.
- The farther bridge bent is visibly darker and less dressed than the mouth. Its dark arch outline is strong in the bridge detail. Stone from the overlapping bridge support remains visible behind that outline, as in the Before projection; this should not be described as a newly open tunnel. The treatment is acceptable here, although that dark ring is a candidate for a later material subtlety pass if close-up presentation becomes more important.
- The chain attachment on the major right-side support, the hanging chains, the entrance keeper bases, banners and lanterns keep the same contacts and positions in the matched images. No floating attachment, newly exposed socket gap, or silhouette clipping is apparent.
- Broad face grain remains cool and readable without a new pale horizontal band. The darker recessed structure does not add orange or compete with the pit and boiler light. Changes are modest in the full-map view and clearer at the actual close views.
- The combined court work makes the entry axis quieter: long gold construction-like guides no longer cross the forecourt, and the larger medallion supplies a single visible destination. The hero and floor-level effects remain legible. This strengthens the reference composition without crowding the approach.

## Reference gap and acceptance

This is a supported improvement in architectural hierarchy, not completion of the reference. The reference still has more irregular stone, deeper joined fortress masses, richer contact shading and a less repetitive perimeter silhouette. The new secondary treatment reduces equally dressed repetition while preserving the established envelope; it cannot by itself deliver the reference's larger sculpted masses.

Accept the image result for this package, subject to the root's final automated and device checks. No source change requested by this review.
