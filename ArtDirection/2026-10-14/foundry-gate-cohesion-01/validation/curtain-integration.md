# Active curtain construction

Baseline: `3474e35`. Scope is the active curtain call block in `FoundryRegionGateEnclosure.cs`, new dedicated `FoundryRegionGateCurtain.cs`, and six focused cases in `FoundryGateCurtainDepthTests.cs` with their Unity meta files. Root captured and sealed 21 Before views before releasing edits.

The upper native curtain previously emitted a complete stone wall and then a flat pointed relief over it. The new active path removes that complete front wall. Broad fitted body stones and a dressed frame partition its former face around the same original opening. A closed backing sits 0.18 design units inside the wall, with five connected returns to the unchanged throat. Both the original outer-frame and inner-opening polygons retain their exact stations. The side wall remains broad masonry; the original closed chamfer and all eight cap triangles are retained. There is no coplanar full wall beneath the new aperture.

The last coping tier becomes a shallow pitched stone roof. Its original bottom bearings and 7.02 maximum ridge remain. Eaves drop 0.08 inside the old envelope; closed gables and side returns connect the roof to its 6.82 bearing. Three broad stone rows and separately owned mortar tile the two slopes. The former full flat roof cap is not retained. Original lower curtain courses, adjacent supporting coping, foundations, footprint/depth owners and sorting remain unchanged.

`GateCurtainWall(stone, far)` is the dedicated active emitter. The root-owned `GateHouseGrain(stone)` runs once after it and before `Create`, so the full new assembly receives the coordinated gate-local mineral field. Existing shared `GateEnclosureTier`, `GateEnclosureRelief`, grain functions and the native outer-buttress fallback are unchanged. This helper adds no texture, material, renderer, shader, per-frame component, gameplay or navigation change. The construction budget allows at most 600 additional vertices per curtain before root's final grain subdivision; actual full-region cost remains a root measurement.

Six focused cases cover both near and far curtains: actual production inclusion, exact retained cap/chamfer and bearing stations, strict independent double-precision visible-backing coverage and overpaint, total front area without a retained duplicate wall, shared local grain, supported coping corners, original projected envelope, ridge/eave witnesses, closed gables and one roof owner. Aperture expectations are independently reconstructed from the old authored dimensions. The area oracle keeps its strict 1e-8 visible-overpaint bound.

Read-only source review before freeze caught a local variable shadowing and an implicitly typed multiple declaration; both were corrected before root compilation. Test coordinates use the original authored gate-offset expressions, avoiding separately rounded decimal literals in exact production-prefix checks. At this note's creation root owns the pending isolated compile, focused/full Unity suites, final graphics and resource measurements. No test pass, visual acceptance, APK or phone acceptance is asserted by this implementation note.
