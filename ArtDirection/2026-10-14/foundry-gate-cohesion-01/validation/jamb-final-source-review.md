## Findings

No blocking runtime issue found in the inspected grain/jamb integration. The remaining acceptance work is the frozen merged capture and measured scene budget, not another source-level brightness or geometry change based only on this review.

- `GateHouseGrain` uses the established fortress patch and density with one translated gate datum. It is called once after the complete carved gate, each active curtain and each outboard landing shoulder, before `Create`. These builders contain static stone. The shared grain helper's animated-metadata guard remains in place.
- The finish changes UVs and splits triangles at mirror folds. Existing positions and colours remain, and inserted colours interpolate the existing triangle field. Original triangle order is preserved as each triangle is replaced in place. The new subdivision therefore does not add another coplanar surface or reorder the jamb's six depth-sorted faces. The field is a fixed-view projected material, not a volumetric stone shader.
- Depth ownership is assigned after subdivision. The carved gate remains inside the enclosing `DepthStructure`; active curtains and landing shoulders have their own `MassDepth` scopes. `Create` assigns the same scope to every appended vertex. There is no uncovered-owner tail or geometry-dependent centroid substitution in these call sites. Baked outer bays, their calibrated material and their half-rank remain separate.
- The jamb partition retains the exact six-corner L-section, base, upper bearing and two concave-cap rectangles. Four course bands and their three mortar beds exhaust each original face; there is no old full wall under them. The shared indexed stone helper retains directional arris light. Jamb depth still comes from the existing L-section; its planar arris rings do not introduce a new three-dimensional recess. Narrow reveal planes remain intentionally darker than exposed planes.
- The local jamb UV map is superseded by the completed-parent field in production, which is deliberate. This adds bounded startup work but no per-frame component. The six jamb tests establish construction ownership before finishing; the three surface cases establish preserved geometry/light, translated field phase and interpolated UV behavior afterward. Tests do not require the old atlas origins, so the actual final field remains testable.

Independent SHA256 checks against the preceding accepted source freeze confirm that `FoundryRegionStoneGrain.cs`, `FoundryRegionGateRecesses.cs`, `FoundryRegionGateHeader.cs`, `FoundryRegionView.cs` and `FoundryRegionGateMasonry.cs` are unchanged. Their shared clipping, indexed arris, depth, header and baked-card behavior is inherited rather than silently modified by this package.
