In both close gate views, this gives the inner piers a clearer relationship to the stronger outer bays accepted in the preceding package. The consolidated upper capital removes an extra projecting horizontal tier below the retained sloping head. Together these are a readable construction change, not merely a surface-density adjustment.

The intentional outward neck flare does not read as a broken or unsupported head in the actual view. The dark pointed recess remains legible, the capital has apparent bearing, and the retained iron collar and adjacent pipe connections show no visible gap. The close view still has substantial upper heads, but they do not introduce a new silhouette or contact defect requiring rejection.

The family grain adjustment is subordinate to the mass change. It preserves the lighting hierarchy and gives the pier surfaces a finer field, but the native stone remains visibly mottled beside the smoother baked outer bays. This package does not establish material parity. The long crown/header also retains its regular, coarse surface response.

The door seal and blue banners remain the gate's focal details. Full-map gains are localized and modest: the entrance reads a little more coherently, without transforming the whole scene. `approach-20-1` crops much of the changed upper architecture and mainly supplies regression evidence; the hero, foreground equipment, route and threshold remain readable. The quiet central floor is retained.
