This is a visible tradeoff, not a claim that stone finish is complete. Keep the connected coordinates and recover restrained fine mineral breakup in a future material pass. Reference gaps remain in small masonry detail, varied edge wear, service-tower massing and the crispness of support-to-equipment transitions.
