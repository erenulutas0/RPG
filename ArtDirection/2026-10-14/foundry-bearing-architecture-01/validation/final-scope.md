# Source scope

This combined package continues from cf511c3. It reshapes native supporting architecture inside existing envelopes: service-tower crowns, workshop shelf bearings and entrance bridge/undercroft surfaces. Map, navigation, camera, encounter data and gameplay are unchanged.

The service crowns replace two dressed outer faces with three connected bearing bands. Shared corner stations preserve the original rim and open mouth, while head-wide mineral sampling and a bounded neutral quarry response unify the shoulder and crown. Source and six new cases belong to DistantWorks and ServiceShaftTests. See service-tower-integration.md.

The four service shelves replace their flat upper strips with a connected recessed bearing profile. Three raked corbel facets retain the old foot/header stations. The two casting cantilevers retain their attachment boxes and original feet, shoulders and caps while using broad knees and the same bearing family. Each foundation, raised piece and cantilever gets one continuous mineral field. Equipment, rails, borrowed images, depth ownership and sort ranks remain. Workshop, CastingBench and LoadingSupportTests own this scope; five existing cases are strengthened and one profile ownership case is added.

The primary entrance bridge's eleven voussoirs now have an inner return, sloping shoulder and dressed face partitioning their original wedge. Open inner edges, outer stations and the straight deck bearing remain. One physical mineral field spans the front and separate soffit, including their shared contacts. Whole-parent grain replaces per-piece restarts on the near island's existing arch bays and responds. Root owns BridgeSupports, FortressSurface, Undercroft and three new FortressHierarchy cases. Existing depth attenuation is unchanged.

No new renderer, material, texture, updater or per-frame work is introduced. Startup geometry and UV generation change within the existing 174000 region / 3600 distant vertex ceilings. Actual capture counts, rather than predicted deltas, determine final resource acceptance. Static counts do not establish device frame time.

Ten new EditMode cases inspect emitted geometry, aperture/foot/rim contacts, actual profile ownership, finite noncollapsed grain and material hierarchy. All prior envelope, opening and rendering-ownership tests remain. Final counts and build status belong to verification.md once the full gate completes.

No phone, ADB, save or telemetry work belongs to this package. Reference parity remains unfinished; the intended gain is more coherent large supporting structures at the existing layout and scale.
