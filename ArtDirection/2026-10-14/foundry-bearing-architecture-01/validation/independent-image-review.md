**Disposition: accept the structural/readability improvement as an incremental package. No blocking visual defect was observed in these views.** There is a visible material tradeoff: the shared parent-plane mapping makes some bridge and shelf stone grain broader and softer than Before. That is not an improvement in fine finish and should remain explicit follow-up work. This package does not establish reference parity.

For the next material pass, retain the new shared mapping and clearer bearings, but recover appropriate stone-scale detail without restarting the same patch per small face. Judge it at portrait and full-map scale beside the already authored gate/machinery. Avoid presenting another small seam or uniform colour lift as closure of the reference gap.
