# Near-island material family scope

Candidate based on fresh `d4a3ecb` Before captures and the approved foundry reference. Reviewed native forecourt-detail and bridge-foot Before views, plus the preceding accepted full-map image. This document records source scope and expected cost; final visual acceptance and executable results belong to the combined package review.

## Change

`FoundryRegionFortressSurface.cs` retunes the existing exposed-bearing RGB factor from `(1.12, 1.09, 1.055)` to `(1.30, 1.25, 1.16)`. Existing uses cover whole upper foundation courses, primary arch fronts, major respond fronts/shoulders and the broad frames of blind panels. Relative to the prior finish, these faces gain about 16.1% red, 14.7% green and 10.0% blue, reducing their blue bias while increasing separation from recesses.

Only two colour arguments change in `FoundryRegionBridgeSupports.cs`: the primary bridge's two foot fronts and straight deck-bearing course now use that same exposed-bearing family. Its arch already uses the family. The paired feet are generated by one existing call in the side loop. Existing haunches, soffits, rear bents and side returns retain their authored tones.

No edit is needed in `FoundryRegionUndercroft.cs`: its existing explicit bearing calls receive the revised local family. Blind-panel backs and returns, arch intrados, lower skirt tones and depth falloff remain unchanged. Keepers, banners, top silhouettes, openings and dark voids receive no new geometry or colour pass.

## Cost and invariants

Expected delta is exactly zero vertices, indices, subdivision calls, UVs, renderer batches, textures, materials, shaders and updater components. The two runtime edits change only colour calculation inputs; they add no position-dependent refinement or mesh operation. Geometry counts must still be measured by the central capture. Existing regional ceilings remain 174000 vertices and 3600 distant vertices; source-level cost neutrality is not device performance evidence.

One existing fortress test file gains a material regression case. It samples the real blind-panel mesh to preserve the accepted RGB values of its upper/lower returns and recessed back, and requires broad exposed-to-recess value separation within a restrained maximum. Existing fortress tests continue to cover apertures, exact bearing stations, face envelopes, grain continuity and coverage. No old tolerance is loosened. Unity execution is reserved for the root integration run.

## Review target

Expect a clearer, more neutral bearing system across the near-island facade and primary bridge, visible in forecourt and full-map views, while recesses and the bridge opening remain quiet. This is a material hierarchy refinement, not new authored form or reference parity. Device motion, HUD, shimmer and timing remain unmeasured.
