## Limits and next direction

These paused, HUD-hidden Editor stills do not establish phone performance, animation, shimmer, crowd occlusion or interactive acceptance. The reference is still substantially richer in integrated stone depth, architectural silhouette and material transitions. This package improves the material vocabulary; it does not establish reference parity. The next bounded art study should address a coherent gate/adjacent masonry module at actual portrait scale, with matched reference and scene cameras, instead of adding unrelated small ornaments.
