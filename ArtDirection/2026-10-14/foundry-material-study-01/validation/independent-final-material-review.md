Accept over V1. The heavy warm mottling that made the rejected hood and trolley look like rough concrete is gone. The corrected trolley reads again as cool cast-metal framing with distinct warmer billets; broad sides remain quiet enough for yokes, bands and wheel silhouette to read. The improvement over the original baseline iron is deliberately modest: local grain is more consistent across the trolley assembly, but the broad faceted hood and simpler native shading remain. It would be misleading to describe this as a wholesale jump to the painted reference's machinery quality. That larger gap needs designed shape-specific light and surface character, rather than more speckled source grain.

In the reviewed stills, that variation stays subordinate to actors and physical stone joints; no obvious new wrap seam was visible. These stills do not establish motion shimmer or phone performance, so those remain device checks rather than assumed passes.
