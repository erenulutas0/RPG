# Next visual priorities after scene cohesion

These are planning recommendations from the final actual-image review, not additional implemented changes or a claim of full reference parity. The accepted pass narrows floor warmth, reduces the scale of mineral streaks, adds a small amount of supported stock and refines lower stone. The next work should produce a visible gain across a whole material or equipment family, rather than consume the remaining budget on repeated tiny trim additions.

1. **One measured workyard station quality proof.** Existing stock/hoist, casting bench and native moulding equipment still differ in silhouette richness, timber scale and iron finish. Select one complete existing station that is clearly visible in both its approach portrait and full-map view, and improve it as a supported assembly within its current footprint. Prefer replacing/reworking existing geometry or one carefully measured authored replacement over adding more separate props. A proof must show convincing contact, readable storage and consistent material scale with its neighbours. Do not accept a detail visible only in a magnified crop, stock hidden by the capture edge, extra floor clutter or a flat dark strip in place of readable stored objects. Record actual texture/material/renderer and geometry costs for any replacement; a bake is not automatically free.

2. **One paving material study at the existing resource budget.** The finer UV span helps, but the raster still has a strong common diagonal grain and the court occupies most of the image. If another floor pass is pursued, compare one restrained alternative mineral treatment on the existing laid flags at the current 1024x1024/ASTC6/mip configuration. Preserve the continuous UV contract, exact flag ownership, quiet central values and current route readability. Aim for short stone inclusions and subdued bedding rather than long shared waves. Accept only a clear native portrait and full-map improvement; avoid solving the remaining texture character with more joints, random slab contrast, extra mesh subdivisions or another broad lighting wash. This is a future material proof, not an instruction to edit raster assets in the closed package.

3. **Near-island material hierarchy without another subdivision pass.** The long native facades, terminal masses and bridge supports remain darker, more regular and more angular than the approved reference, while the baked gate bays have softer authored form. First compare the largest connected faces as a single family and adjust existing face value/material scale where it improves bearing, recess and return readability. Keep voids dark, keep top contacts visible, and preserve the keeper/bridge silhouettes and openings. Spend effort on an obvious broad-form improvement, not more narrow courses, black recess lines or fine triangulation. If a replacement proof is chosen, replace one bounded existing face assembly and measure its total cost before generalizing it.

## Retained limits and acceptance boundary

The region is 36 renderers / 172735 vertices, including 3572 distant vertices. Ceilings remain 174000 / 3600, leaving only 1265 region and 28 distant vertices. Treat that headroom as a constraint, not a target to spend. Prefer resource-neutral changes or replacements that recover geometry. Adaptive floor-light sampling can change vertex count even when no explicit primitive is added. Static resource totals do not establish device frame time; no performance improvement may be inferred from a smaller mesh alone.

Preserve the map, walking union, collision/navigation, camera, actor depth and clear combat floor. Existing immortal-crowd waypoint congestion remains unresolved; its evidence is `ArtDirection/2026-10-12/foundry-forecourt-layout-01/validation/crowd-diagnostic.md`.
