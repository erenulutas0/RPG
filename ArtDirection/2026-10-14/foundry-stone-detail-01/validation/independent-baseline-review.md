# Independent baseline review

Baseline: `d9f3332`. Read-only review; no source changes, Unity, build, Git or device actions by this reviewer.

I reopened the sealed `before/bridge-foot.png`, `service-shaft-detail.png` and `full-map.png`, and the approved `2026-09-23/foundry-layout-01/foundry-v1.png`. The layout and landmark hierarchy are established, but this is still visibly simpler and less materially convincing than the reference. The close bridge has broad soft mineral clouds and large blank spandrels; the service shafts retain tall flat fields. At full-map scale the open court and regular perimeter also remain substantially more uniform than the reference. This package can improve stone construction and material scale, not establish reference parity.

The wall material uses the imported authored `FoundryMasonryV4` texture through the existing masonry material. It is not a startup-generated noise texture. Its protected interior patches contain usable small mineral detail; fitting one patch over a whole parent structure enlarges that detail into the soft cloudlike field visible in the baseline. More sparse vertex tint cannot restore that missing frequency. An importer resolution increase would not correct the world-space scale and would affect every consumer.

The bounded root approach is appropriate: reuse the authored texture, retain a continuous parent-coordinate field, mirror inside its mortar-free patch, and partition triangles at every fold before batching. Review must confirm physical density inside triangles, not just UV values at vertices. Retain silhouette/contact positions, painter order, interpolated vertex colour, unrelated ranges and fixed resource counts. Scope subdivision to the final complete parent; static metadata must be rejected explicitly. Existing below-floor ranks and explicitly owned raised shelves avoid unscoped depth-owner centroid changes.

The companion changes are coherent: a few broad fitted courses across the primary bridge spandrel, and shallow indexed blind fields within the service shafts' existing pier rectangles. They should remain subordinate to the arch/opening. The visual gate is actual before/after evidence: fine stone should read without mirror-chevron repetition, loud seams, a new brick overlay, loss of the bridge aperture, or darker flattened shafts. Check both close detail and portrait scale; added geometry alone is not acceptance.
