**Verdict: accept as an incremental material/construction improvement. No new visual blocker found in these static views. This is not reference parity or device acceptance.**

- **Bridge:** The largest visible improvement. The previous broad cloudy mineral patches on the arch ring and feet now read as finer mottled stone. Broad horizontal beds and staggered joints articulate the formerly blank triangular spandrels, without turning the entire arch into thin stripes. The inner return remains dark and distinct, and both the large lower aperture and deck contact stay open/continuous. The entry portrait shows the same improvement at gameplay framing. Some texture rhythm can still be noticed when studying the enlarged detail, but there is no dominant mirrored-chevron pattern or obvious UV fold crack in the inspected capture.
- **Service shafts:** The new broad inset fields on the bay piers create a more deliberate bearing/recess hierarchy. Their contribution is subtle at full-map scale and clearer in the workyard portrait. Existing arched fire bays, neck and crown remain recognizable; I do not see an accidentally sealed opening or a new projecting obstruction. These remain fairly simple, dark towers rather than the reference's richly varied foundry stacks.
- **Stock/casting shelves and near-island masonry:** Finer stone grain carries more consistently across the lower bearing faces and corbels. The stock close-up makes the replacement of enlarged soft texture particularly clear. Shelf decks, equipment feet and rails remain seated as before. The forecourt's undercroft receives a quieter version of the same finer material; it is not a large silhouette change. The retained shade function is sampled at additional vertices, so this is not a claim that final rendered lighting is bit-identical.
- **Whole composition:** The gate, pit, workyard, open court, entrance route and background hierarchy read as before. No new clutter competes with the hero or removes an open lane in these views. Full-map improvement is modest; most of the gain is visible in nearby architecture. The more detailed painted cavern still contrasts with the simpler foreground assets.
