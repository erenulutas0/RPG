## Main visual finding

The baseline's closed door has detailed painted stone and metal, but the tall flanking jambs and their shoulders still read as broad smooth monoliths. Their silhouettes and true recessed bays are useful; the missing part is the visible scale and construction of the stone around those bays. A few broad fitted courses can connect this architecture to the reference without weakening the existing pointed recesses or adding smaller decorations.

The previous cap joints and recessed lintel already improve the top bearing. Preserve those, the new clear door threshold and both supported braziers. The current front door is closed; a clear threshold is not a new route.

## Proposed package assessment

1. Broad courses on tall jambs/shoulders are the strongest local improvement. Keep the real opening, quarter-depth splay, backing and return stations fixed. Course joints should stop at their correct stone/face boundaries. Avoid a regular small-brick grid or repeated bright outlines.
2. Replace the curtain's retained full face plus overlaid ashlar with one surface partition. This has value beyond close-up appearance: it removes ambiguous coplanar ownership and can keep the mineral field continuous. Match existing broad stone size, shared weathering and local forge lighting; do not restart a conspicuous atlas tile per block.
3. A restrained shared lower-buttress material treatment is a reasonable third task. Prefer support/crown contact and mineral bedding across the existing face over extra block geometry, dark individual tiles or new props. Existing stone contacts already exist, so a new pass must justify itself visibly rather than stacking another uniform darkening.

## Acceptance conditions for final captures

Compare matched gate detail, crown detail, portrait approach and full map. Stone courses should read at gate-detail scale, while the portrait still prioritizes the hero, door emblem and flames. No new cracks, gaps, detached shoulders or widened envelopes. The niche depth should remain evident, mortar should not become stripes, and the overall gate should not become materially darker without purpose. The full-map composition should remain stable.

This is a local construction/material improvement. The reference remains denser and more deliberately composed at the scale of the whole map. Gate work alone cannot establish overall reference parity. Motion, shimmer, crowds and phone performance cannot be accepted from static Editor captures.
