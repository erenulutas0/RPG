# Reflected forge light on industrial metal — 2026-10-09

The next visual package connects the boiler iron, pressure mains/collars and reservoir courses to the same authored forge sources that already light the stone. Source position and height are sampled in design coordinates with the gate surface elevation. Existing directional metal shading is multiplied by bounded reflected warmth; fire-front textures and glow windows remain untouched. The effect falls off above each source and outside its reach, rather than making whole towers orange.

Long lit pipes are subdivided at at most 0.75 projected units so a warm source between their ends is represented. Their radius/cross section, source texture, material, depth ownership and draw batches are preserved. This is static vertex-color illumination at scene build, not real-time lighting or per-frame mesh work. Generic crane rods, valve wheels and unrelated iron keep their prior treatment.
