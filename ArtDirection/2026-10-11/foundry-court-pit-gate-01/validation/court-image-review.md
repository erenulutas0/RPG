# Actual court image review

Read-only inspection of matched Unity `before/` and `after/` images using `view_image`: `court-detail.png`, `approach-0-0.png`, `full-map.png`, plus `shaft-detail.png` for the adjacent combined pit change. Assets remained frozen.

Accepted as a bounded visual improvement, with no visible blocking rendering defect in these captures.

- **Court detail:** the compass now reads as eight worn brass inserts with alternating faces, rather than an empty outline. The continuous boundary is clearer, while the broad paving between the arms remains exposed. The fitted inner flags and their shallow joins are visible under the symbol; the former uninterrupted dark centre is gone. The outer ring and adjacent paving meet without visible gaps.
- **Portrait approach:** hero, chest and enemies still separate clearly from the subdued brown metal. The blue gameplay ring remains the brighter signal. The engraving is more solid close up, but does not read as glowing gold. Its centre is partly hidden by the hero in this fixture, so this frame cannot evaluate every centre junction simultaneously.
- **Whole map:** the compass is now identifiable as a small central star where the earlier outline largely disappeared. This is a modest local readability gain; the overall map size, silhouette, composition and resemblance to the target reference have not changed in this slice. The small fitted flag joints intentionally disappear at this scale.
- **Adjacent pit:** the new upper bearing course and long rib shoulders are visibly connected under the existing collar. The three furnace columns remain open; both vessel silhouettes and molten pool are intact. The new upper course is darker and heavier than before, but the masonry still reads and the visible returns do not cross the vessel faces. This is more substantial depth articulation at close range, with only a small full-map effect.

Limits: these are matched static Unity captures, not device motion/performance evidence. They do not prove shimmer-free movement, crowded-combat readability or final reference-quality art. The court remains stylized and its materials simpler than the approved painting; this package should not be described as finishing the reference match.
