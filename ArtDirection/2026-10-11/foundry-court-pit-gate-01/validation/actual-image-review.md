## Limits and next art work

This is not full reference parity. The map silhouette, extent and layout are unchanged; whole-map gains are local. The reference has richer stone variation, wear, material integration and more varied industrial construction. Gate recess backs are nearly black at distance, and the pit rib atlas has broad, soft rivets in close view. A coordinated material pass is more useful next than additional isolated trim bands. Preserve the quiet court and readable actor space while addressing those differences.

Static captures do not establish moving-camera shimmer, crowd/HUD readability, device performance or midrange-phone acceptance. No ADB or installation occurred in this package. The earlier concrete restart/install decision is still pending.
