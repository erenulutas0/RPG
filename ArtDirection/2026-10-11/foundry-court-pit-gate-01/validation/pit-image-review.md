The upper wall reads as a carried assembly, rather than a flat texture meeting the railing. The outer pair remains behind the two vessel silhouettes, and neither hood is cut into by the new geometry. The molten bottom, orange wall foot and contained fire aperture remain readable. No new bright strip spills onto the walking floor.

The existing narrow black strip at the outer corner, rail/post contacts and surrounding floor-light shape are also present before this package; no new gap or overlap is apparent there.
- At whole-map scale the pit remains the same compact warm landmark. The added vertical structure makes the lining marginally stronger, but the change is **local and modest** in the complete composition. It does not establish full reference fidelity or change the pit's footprint/proportions.

Remaining visual limitations: the reused strap texture produces relatively broad, soft rivets on the new rib faces in close view. The new deep course has a warm brown cast compared with the cooler rail; it is coherent with the heated lining but is still simpler than the reference's varied dressed stone and iron construction. No extra trim pass is needed to approve this slice. A future material-quality pass should address that texture scale together with the vessel surfaces rather than adding more isolated bands.

These three static views do not verify moving silhouettes, all near/far approach angles, HUD/crowd play, shimmer, device performance or midrange hardware. Physical-device acceptance remains separate.
