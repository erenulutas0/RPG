# Independent gate source review

Reviewed the candidate `FoundryRegionGateRecesses.cs` and the current Architecture, Enclosure and Header diffs against their existing helpers. Read-only review; no Unity, ADB or runtime edits. No blocking source defect found.

- **Inward directions:** `ArchitecturalTier` emits octagon edge 0 first (the south/v0 face), then edge 6 (the west/u0 face), then edge 7 (the near clipped corner). `GateRecessedShaft` therefore correctly translates the first backing in +v and the second in +u. These translations go inside the original prism, rather than outward into the approach. Both .28 and .36 depth choices leave the opening well away from the opposite face; the copied near chamfer can mask the small projected overlap of an inset backing at the corner.
- **Taper and boundary ownership:** Front points are taken directly from `LitFoundryTier`, then interpolated through `GateFaceLerp`; they follow its real taper rather than a separate guessed face plane. The opening/frame/border pentagons are nested and convex. Their five matching strips tile the original face, including the upper shoulder divisions and the shared centre of the top border. The original corner and cap vertices/UVs are copied unchanged from index 8 onward.
- **No hidden full wall:** The local shell's first eight vertices are used only to recover the two wall shapes. They are not emitted into the destination. The replacement calls remove the former complete `GateEnclosureTier` shaft and its planar lancet overlay. Niche backing is confined to the translated inner pentagon; front masonry is the surrounding ring. No full-face rectangle remains across either new recess.
- **Painter order:** Each face emits backing, then five depth returns, then the outer bearing/frame strips. Thus the nearby front stones mask projected hidden parts of the returns. The two faces preserve `ArchitecturalTier`'s order; the original clipped corner and cap follow both. Existing far/near gate batches and depth owners are unchanged.
- **Header and lock:** The flanking coping is consolidated within the original outer footprint and the same `height + 1.16` maximum. Its base still sits on the continuous lintel. The central socket, cartouche outline, compressed rise mapping, backing plane and iron lock are unchanged; existing lock-in-backing regression coverage remains relevant. The side masses stop at v=3.35 and 6.65, preserving the central socket interval.

The new tests inspect projected coverage and the original prism silhouette, but this review does not claim their results. Root should inspect actual near/far pier portraits for readable throat depth, the front chamfer masking the far return, and the widened header shoulders around the lock. The underlying masonry change is substantial; exact visual similarity and whether the recesses become too dark remain image-review decisions, not source guarantees.
