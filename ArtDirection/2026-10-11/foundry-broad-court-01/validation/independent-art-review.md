**Decision: accept this widening and equipment package. No visual blocker identified in the inspected captures.**

- The western wing is visibly broader relative to the unchanged pit, bridge and gate. The additional usable-looking floor and relocated perimeter demonstrate an actual layout change rather than a smaller camera scale.
- The turntable, extended freight tracks, trolley and mould bench still form a coherent working area. Their larger surrounding spaces look traversable; no disconnected rail endpoint, floor hole, floating equipment or broken perimeter contact is apparent.
- The trolley's staggered secured load and the mould bench's warmer top improve their separate identities. Equipment remains subordinate to the pit lighting and hero rather than becoming a bright second focal point.
- Hero feet and immediate floor remain readable in all three new portraits. The bench and trolley contacts appear seated, and the inspected views show no new painter-order defect.
- Retaining the rear casting shelf is acceptable. The larger interval from the shifted stock station is visually mediated by the north tower and balustrade. The mould portrait still relates the interior station to the rear casting equipment. Closing that gap is not necessary to accept this package.

Reference fidelity is still incomplete. This widens the industrial wing; it does not reproduce every overall proportion of the concept. The gate causeway and depth remain elongated relative to its broader central composition. Equipment and architectural forms remain simpler, the floor course pattern more regular, and the workyard less densely authored than the reference. Further work should address those larger composition/material differences rather than claim completion from width alone.
