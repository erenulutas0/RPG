**Decision: accept the bounded silhouette improvement. No introduced visual blocker found in these captures.**

- The tall shaft's narrowing shoulder and open exhaust crown now differ clearly from the shorter shaft's service roof and chimney. This distinction survives the full-map view, although its contribution remains localized.
- Roofs, crown bases, shoulders, and buttresses appear continuously supported. No floating cap, detached buttress, obvious contact gap, or newly inverted painter ordering was visible.
- The broken span has less uniform ends and a clearer taper in the skyline detail. Its fracture benefit is subtle at full-map scale; it remains a deliberately simplified, angular structure.
- Stone and recess values remain quiet relative to the foreground lamps, equipment, and hero. No new checker pattern, conspicuous texture seam, upper transparency, or actor obstruction was observed.

This does not establish reference-level completion. The larger remaining gap is the composition of the workyard: the reference groups production equipment into a broad working terrace, while the runtime still concentrates much of it on narrow perimeter shelves. A future substantial slice should address that grouping and circulation, with an explicit navigation contract for any newly occupied floor. Another small distant-material or trim pass would have lower value.

This review covers static visual evidence only. It makes no independent claim about vertex counts, runtime performance, motion, navigation, tests, or Android rendering; those remain separate validation gates.
