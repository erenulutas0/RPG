# Next visual target after paving V4

Comparison reviewed against ArtDirection/2026-09-23/foundry-layout-01/foundry-v1.png and the current actual Unity overview and portrait. This is a prioritisation note, not implemented content or a promise of reference equivalence.

The composition reads as the same foundry family: court, left pit, south bridge, northeast gate and boiler landmarks. The surface update reduces directional streaks, but does not change architecture, silhouette, material lighting or the portrait camera. Further small floor texture passes have diminishing value.

The next preferred art study is the paired forge-keeper statues at the bridge head. The approved concept has large hooded guardian silhouettes; the current game has narrow posts topped with fire baskets. Unlike another trim/detail pass, those figures could change the entrance identity at a glance. Keep the existing safe walkway width and structures' ground footprints. First compare an actual-game-camera silhouette/painted asset proof before runtime implementation. Require original design, readable large folds, restrained bronze tools, anchored contact shade, correct actor depth/cutaway, and no hero-like blue glow. Do not scatter additional small props around the combat court.

A painted cutout or simple mesh with a dedicated painted atlas is a candidate, not a settled implementation. It must be evaluated against the current 17 region-renderer budget; do not quietly raise that test limit or claim a free sprite implementation. Device portrait framing must show the asset during ordinary movement, not only in a promotional overview. If both statues crop out during normal entry, fix the asset/framing choice at the proof stage.

Other remaining gaps: boiler silhouette variety and integrated surface lighting, the repeated long foundation piers, and the overall density/scale of floor flags relative to the concept. These are larger art/layout choices. None is resolved by this paving texture or the earlier flame/banner animations.
